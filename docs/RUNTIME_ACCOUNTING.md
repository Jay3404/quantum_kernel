# Runtime accounting

This document defines the record-reconstructed partial time components in
the selected-model component summary. All times are seconds. Each table entry is the arithmetic mean over
the ten seeds10,20,...,100 for its dataset, kernel and method.

## Source records and calculation

`results/runtime/seed_components.csv` supplies the per-seed values. The original
implementation is retained as `code/analyze_professor_revision.py`;
its `cold_accounting(choice)` function constructs the following quantities:

```text
cold_training_component_sum_s
  = selected_training_recorded_s
    - state_train_incremental_s
    + state_train_cold_s

cold_test_component_sum_s
  = selected_test_recorded_s
    - state_test_incremental_s
    + state_test_cold_s
```

The selected training/test fields come from `choice["training_seconds"]` and
`choice["test_inference_seconds"]`. The incremental state fields come from
`representation_details["state_seconds_by_part"]`, with `train` for training
and `gate` for test. Their cold replacements come from the corresponding
`measured_cold_state_seconds_by_part` entries. The procedure replaces the
recorded incremental state-generation component with the recorded initial
state-generation component. It leaves the other recorded components unchanged.

`fit_s` is the SVC fitting component. `state_train_cold_s` is the initial
training-state component. The CSV also retains train/validation/test Gram and
decision components and the state-cache flag. For the RBF entries, state-vector
components are zero. The standalone runtime replay checks these identities and
reconstructs the eight the selected-model component summary rows from the supplied CSV.

## Measurement scope

The Cold sums are partial reconstructions, not complete end-to-end timings.
They exclude preprocessing, risk estimation, the complete hyperparameter search,
and some circuit or auxiliary-representation preparation. Exact double-precision
statevectors are generated by CPU Aer; GPU devices perform Gram algebra.
Physical-device shots, communication and queueing are outside these simulator
records. No new timing measurement was performed for this editorial revision.

Standalone XGBoost's inference field combines validation and test prediction;
the SVM inference component is test-only. The manuscript reports their scopes
separately and computes no speedup ratio from these differently scoped values.
Risk-estimator preparation includes repeated OOF preprocessing, fitting and
prediction, together with the full-training risk estimator. Selected reference
fitting, risk preparation and complete campaign wall time are separate records.
